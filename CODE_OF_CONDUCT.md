# Code Of Conduct

`agentic-homelab` is for practical, safety-first collaboration around personal
infrastructure.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume homelabs contain sensitive personal systems and data.
- Do not pressure users to share secrets, private IPs, logs, screenshots, or
  diagnostics that may expose their infrastructure.
- Prefer safe, reversible, well-explained changes.
- Clearly label speculation, uncertainty, and risk.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Encouraging users to run destructive commands without context, backup checks,
  rollback, and approval.
- Requesting or posting real credentials, private keys, tokens, cookies, recovery
  codes, or sensitive network details.
- Publishing another person's private homelab details.

## Reporting

For security-sensitive issues, use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). For conduct
issues, contact the maintainers through the repository owner profile or a private
channel if one is available.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation to protect users and
the project.
